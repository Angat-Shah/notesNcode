CONTRIBUTING.md
A Commitment to Academic Excellence
Thank you for your interest in contributing to notesNcode. This repository exists as a curated archive of B.Tech CSE documentation, and its value is maintained through the collective rigor of its contributors.

We welcome contributions that align with our mission: providing clear, accurate, and well-structured resources for the engineering community. By contributing, you are helping to refine a legacy of knowledge for those who follow.

Standards of Contribution
To maintain the integrity of the archive, we ask that all contributions adhere to the following benchmarks:

1. Accuracy & Clarity
Technical Precision: Ensure that code implementations are functional, commented, and follow best practices for the respective language.

Academic Integrity: Notes should be synthesized in your own words. We do not host plagiarized content or unauthorized proprietary materials.

2. Structural Consistency
Organization: Please follow the existing directory structure (e.g., Semester/Subject/Type).

Naming Conventions: Use clear, descriptive file names (e.g., DAA_Lab_Manual_v1.pdf instead of doc1.pdf).

3. Quality of Documentation
Formatting: Written notes should be legible, ideally in Markdown or searchable PDF formats.

Context: When adding new resources, provide a brief description of the content to help users understand its relevance.

The Contribution Process
We follow a standard professional workflow to ensure every change is reviewed with care:

Fork & Clone: Create a personal fork of the repository to begin your work.

Branching: Use a descriptive branch name (e.g., add/semester-5-os-notes or fix/algo-lab-logic).

Commit Messages: Use clear, imperative commit messages (e.g., "Refactor: Update Sorting Algorithm implementations in C++").

Pull Request (PR): Submit a PR with a concise summary of your changes. Mention why these additions or corrections are valuable to the archive.

Our Philosophy on Collaboration
At notesNcode, we believe that documentation is a form of engineering in itself. Whether you are correcting a typographical error in a lab manual or adding an entirely new subject archive, your attention to detail ensures that this resource remains a benchmark for others.

We appreciate your time, your intellect, and your dedication to the open-source academic community.
